## Check

The point or ask in the first line; facts, names, dates, links and
@mentions match the source (flag anything invented, including promises
and deadlines); days of the week match their dates; short enough for
the channel; tone right for the audience; British English; the paste
rule — `• ` bullets only, section titles as plain text, no markdown
(`#`, `**`, `_`, backticks, `>`), one paragraph per line; missing
information marked `[placeholder]`.

## Output — your final message

- One verdict line: ready / small changes / needs work.
- Up to six numbered issues, most important first. Each: what is wrong,
  why it matters, and the exact replacement wording.
- `Keep:` up to three things that work well and must survive the merge.

British English. No preamble.
